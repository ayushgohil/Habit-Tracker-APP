# Code of Conduct

Ember is for everyone who wants to build better habits, and everyone who wants to help build the app. We want taking part to be a good experience whatever your age, background, experience or language.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intentions and ask when something is unclear.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Reviews are about the code.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone here was a beginner once.
- Keep discussions on topic and use clear, respectful language.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information without their permission.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about who someone is
- Sexual or violent language and images
- Spam, trolling, or repeated pushing after a decision has been made
- Anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. Serious or repeated problems can lead to a permanent ban.

To report a problem, email the maintainer at ayush.gohil12@gmail.com or use GitHub's "Report content" option. Reports are handled privately.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests, and anywhere else you represent the project.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
